Camerae

Every highway camera on your route, in the order you'll pass them.

Camerae opens on a map of the highway cameras around you; tap one to watch it live. Enter a start and a destination, and Camerae finds every traffic camera along the drive, with live video where the state provides it. Check snow on the pass, a backup at the bridge or fog in the valley before you leave.

Coverage

California (Caltrans) and Nevada (NDOT): about 4,000 cameras, over half of them with live video.

Illinois (IDOT, the Illinois Tollway, and Lake, DuPage and Kane counties): about 3,800 camera views, snapshots only.

Common questions

A camera shows "OFFLINE". The state is serving its "camera unavailable" image for that camera. These usually come back on their own.

A camera I know about isn't on my route. Camerae only lists cameras within about 150 m of the road you'll drive, and skips cameras on overpasses and crossing roads. If one is missing that you think should show, open an issue with the route and the camera's name.

"Current Location" doesn't work. Allow location in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camerae.

Is it safe to use while driving? No. Camerae is for planning a trip and for passengers. Don't watch cameras while you drive.

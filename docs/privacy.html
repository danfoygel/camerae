Privacy Policy

Camerae for iPhone · Effective November 18, 2026

Camerae shows the highway cameras along a driving route. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store or receive any information about you.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Your saved Home and recent places are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your location, if you allow it, is used on your device to start a route from where you are and to show your position on the map. It is never sent to the developer.

Services the app talks to

To work, Camerae connects directly from your iPhone to these services. The developer has no server in between and does not see these requests.

  • Apple Maps. Place searches, and the start and end points of a route (including your current location if you choose it), are sent to Apple to provide suggestions and directions. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub. The app downloads an updated list of cameras from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) a few times a day. The request carries no information about you, but GitHub receives your device's IP address, as any website would. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Caltrans and the Nevada Department of Transportation. Camera snapshots and live video load straight from their public servers. Like any website, those servers receive your device's IP address and the camera being requested. See the Caltrans Conditions of Use and Nevada 511 for their policies.
  • Illinois camera services. Illinois camera snapshots load straight from Travel Midwest (travelmidwest.com), IDOT's traveler information service, and from Lake County PASSAGE (lakecountypassage.com). Like any website, those servers receive your device's IP address and the camera being requested.

What the developer does not do

  • Collect personal information, location history or usage data
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Sell or share any data

Children

Camerae does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
